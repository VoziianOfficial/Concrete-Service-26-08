Overview

This Cookie Notice explains the limited browser storage used by StoneDrop Concrete. The site is designed to keep storage simple and focused on basic functionality, not advertising profiles.

Cookies and local storage are small records saved by your browser. They can help a site remember preferences, keep forms working, support security, or measure basic technical performance.

Essential Storage

The cookie notice uses local browser storage to remember that you accepted the message. This prevents the compact card from appearing on every page load.

This preference record is considered essential because it supports a cleaner browsing experience and does not require you to create an account or submit additional information.

Form Functionality

The contact form sends the information you enter to the local form handler so your concrete request can be reviewed. The form may rely on standard browser behavior and basic technical checks to support delivery and reduce misuse.

The form does not require advertising cookies. Do not include sensitive information in the message field unless it is needed for your concrete service request.

No Advertising Cookies

The site does not use advertising cookies, behavioral retargeting cookies, or third-party ad pixels as part of the current experience.

If analytics, maps, embedded media, or other third-party services are added later, this notice may be updated to explain what storage those services use and why.

Managing Preferences

You can clear local site data in your browser settings. If cleared, the cookie notice may appear again on your next visit.

Most browsers also allow you to block or delete cookies, inspect stored site data, and set privacy preferences. Blocking all storage may affect small convenience features such as the acceptance notice.

We may update this Cookie Notice when site functionality changes, when storage practices change, or when legal or operational requirements require clearer explanation.
