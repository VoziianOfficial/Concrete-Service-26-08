Overview

StoneDrop Concrete operates as a concrete service request and coordination website. This policy explains how information submitted through the site is collected, used, protected, and shared when you ask about installation, repair, resurfacing, driveways, patios, slabs, or commercial concrete work.

We aim to collect only the information needed to understand your project request and respond in a practical way. We do not sell personal information.

Information We Collect

When you submit a request, we collect the name, email address, selected service, and message you provide. Your message may include project details such as surface type, approximate size, site access, timing, condition of existing concrete, and preferred finish.

We may also receive basic technical information such as browser type, device category, referring page, and site interaction data needed for security, diagnostics, spam prevention, and performance.

How We Use Information

We use submitted information to review your concrete service request, respond by email, prepare a clearer project brief, and improve the site experience. We may organize your details into practical categories so the next conversation can focus on scope, access, materials, timing, and finish expectations.

We may also use information to maintain site security, troubleshoot form delivery, confirm consent records such as cookie notice acceptance, and understand which service pages are most useful to visitors.

Service Coordination And Sharing

If a request requires contractor follow-up, details may be shared only as needed to evaluate availability, scope, and next steps. Shared details are limited to information relevant to the requested concrete service.

Final contractor terms, pricing, site visits, permits, warranties, insurance, scheduling, and project performance are handled directly before work begins. We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect the site from misuse.

Retention And Security

We keep request information only for as long as reasonably needed to respond, coordinate follow-up, maintain records, resolve disputes, and improve service quality. Retention periods may vary based on the nature of the request and operational needs.

No website can guarantee perfect security, but we use reasonable administrative and technical measures to reduce unauthorized access, misuse, loss, or alteration of submitted information.

Your Choices

You can choose not to submit the form. You may also ask us to review, correct, or delete information associated with your request when legally and operationally appropriate.

The site uses a small local cookie preference record so the cookie notice does not reappear after acceptance. You can clear local site data in your browser settings. See the Cookies page for more detail.
